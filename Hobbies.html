A mix of sport and tinkering. Here's where my time goes when I'm not measuring steel.

01

Football

The sport I keep coming back to. I love playing a casual game whenever I get the chance, and I'm just as happy watching — following the big games and arguing about them afterwards is half the fun.

FootballPlaying & watching

Padel

A newer addiction. Fast, social, and a good excuse to be competitive without taking it too seriously. Easy to get into, surprisingly hard to get good at.

3D printing

I've got a Bambu Lab A1 and use it as a practical tool more than anything — I'm no expert at 3D modelling, but it's been genuinely useful. I designed and printed the inspection rig that made my thesis measurements possible, plus the odd mount or bracket when something needs one.

Bambu Lab A1OpenSCADPractical prints

PC gaming

I built my own gaming PC and spend a good chunk of my downtime on it. Picking the parts and putting it together was half the fun — the other half is everything I've played on it since.

GamingBuilt my own rig
05

Arduino & Elegoo kits

I like working through Elegoo kits and building small Arduino projects — wiring up sensors and displays, writing the sketch, then printing an enclosure so it ends up as something real on a shelf. A recent one was a little climate monitor for my gaming room.
